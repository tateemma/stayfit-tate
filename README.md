# StayFit Tate - Personal App

An offline-first PWA (installable on your phone) that tracks your workouts, gym calendar, habits, body weight and progress photos. No hosting cost — hosted free on GitHub Pages. No app-store account needed. Optional free backup to your own Google Drive.

## What's inside

- **Today** — shows your scheduled workout for the day (Full Body / Upper Push / Upper Pull / Lower Body), with cardio blocks, supersets, rest timers, habit checkboxes, and a live calorie estimate.
- **Calendar** — checkmark for every day you logged a session, plus totals for the month and all-time.
- **Progress** — body weight log with a trend chart, and a body-photo gallery.
- **Programs** — browse all 4 workouts and their "why" notes (which muscles/fat they target).
- **Settings** — edit your weekly schedule, cardio defaults (minutes/incline/resistance), default bodyweight for calorie estimates, and Google Drive backup.

Everything is stored locally in the browser (IndexedDB), so it works with **no internet connection** at the gym. Google Drive backup is optional and only needed when you want to sync/restore.


## Notes on the workout program

The 4 sessions are adapted from your old trainer's Trainerize program to the equipment at your current gym (Matrix cable/functional trainer, dumbbells, barbell + plates, kettlebells, plyo boxes, bench) — same training intensity and structure (supersets, rest periods, rep ranges), different machines where the old gym had dedicated machines this gym doesn't (e.g. seated leg extension/abduction/chest-fly machines → cable trainer or dumbbell equivalents).

Cardio (20 min treadmill walk + 20 min elliptical, incline/resistance 4/4/5/5) is fixed into every session and fully editable in Settings — change it any time as your fitness improves.
